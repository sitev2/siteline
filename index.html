        Practice

        A new random maze every time. No guns: find the exit door and get out. Something else is in there with you: walking is quiet, sprinting (Shift) is not. Watch out for the holes in the floor. Friends can join you from the pause menu.

        Settings

        Click an action, then press the key you want. Esc cancels. The mouse wheel switches guns and the right mouse button scopes as well.
