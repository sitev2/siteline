                  How to play

                  The round

                  Attackers carry a bomb. Plant it on the bomb site, then protect it until it explodes.

                  Defenders stop the plant, or defuse the bomb once it's down. Wipe out the other team to win the round too.

                  Practice & bots: a practice range with bot targets, a 2v2 bot match and bot deathmatch, on easy, normal or hard. Friends can join from the invite in the pause menu and take a bot's place.

                  Deathmatch (2–4 players): everyone for themselves, first to 30 kills or 10 minutes. You respawn after a second with full armour; press B / 🛒 any time to pick any gun for free.

                  Teams of two. No respawns during a round. First to 9 rounds wins, the teams swap sides after round 8, and 8–8 is a draw. Rounds last 1:55; once the bomb is planted it explodes after 40 seconds. Defusing takes 10 seconds, or 5 with a defuse kit — keep looking at the bomb.

                  Money & buying

                  Everyone starts each half with $800. You earn money for winning rounds, for kills (cheaper guns pay more) and a growing loss bonus when your team loses. Buy guns, armour and kits in your spawn area during the first 20 seconds of each round. Survivors keep their gear.

                  Shooting

                  Stand still to be accurate: guns only shoot straight once you've slowed to about a third of your speed — tap the opposite key to stop instantly. Automatic guns kick in a fixed pattern you can learn. Armour stops part of the damage and stops your aim being knocked around. Bullets pass through boxes, crates and barrels with reduced damage; solid walls stop everything.

                  Crouching & teamwork

                  Crouch to be quieter, smaller and recover your aim faster; crouch in mid-air to jump higher. Jump onto a teammate's head to reach high spots — they can walk around with you on top. The dead drop their gun (and kit) for anyone to pick up.

                  Phone & tablet

                  Turn your device sideways. Put your left thumb anywhere on the left side to move. Drag on the right side to aim.

                  Hold the big fire button to shoot — you can keep dragging it to aim while firing. ⇄ switches guns (and the bomb, if you carry it), ◎ scopes (or switches burst mode), ⤓ crouches (tap again to stand), ⇩ drops your gun, 🛒 opens the buy menu. ✋ appears when you can plant, defuse or pick up a gun.

                  🔍 inspects your gun, 💬 opens chat and 🔦 switches your flashlight on at night. You can move, resize and fade every button in Settings → Phone layout.

                  On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen and launch from there for full screen.
